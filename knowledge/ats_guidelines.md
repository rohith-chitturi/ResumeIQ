# ATS Guidelines

## Formatting
- Do not use tables, columns, or text boxes. ATS systems parse top-to-bottom, left-to-right.
- Stick to standard fonts: Arial, Calibri, Times New Roman.
- Ensure the file is saved as a standard PDF or DOCX.

## Headers
- Use standard section headers: "Work Experience", "Education", "Skills". Avoid creative headers like "Where I've Been" as the ATS will fail to map the section correctly.

## Date Formats
- Use clear date formats: "MM/YYYY - MM/YYYY" or "Month YYYY - Month YYYY".
- "Present" is acceptable for current roles.
